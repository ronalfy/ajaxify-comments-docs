## Finding the Admin Settings From the Plugins Screen <a href="#finding-the-admin-settings-from-the-plugins-screen" id="finding-the-admin-settings-from-the-plugins-screen"></a>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/dlxplugins-1695279456588-1x.webp" alt=""><figcaption><p>Ajaxify Comments Plugin Row</p></figcaption></figure>

If you are on the plugin's screen, search for **Ajaxify Comments** and click on the `Settings` link.

### Finding the Admin Settings from the Menu

Alternatively, in the admin menu, head to `Settings->Ajaxify Comments`.
